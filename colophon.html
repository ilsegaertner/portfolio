Colophon

How this site is made.

Type

Text is set in Source Serif 4, designed by Frank Grießhammer for Adobe, with old-style figures and small caps for abbreviations. Navigation, dates and labels are set in Geist by Vercel. Both are variable fonts under the SIL Open Font License and are served from this domain.

Layout

Every page has a reading column of about 66 characters and a narrow margin to its left for headings and years, the way side notes sit in a printed book. There are three colors: paper, ink and one indigo.

Build

The pages are static HTML, assembled with Vite from a few shared partials and styled with Tailwind CSS. They ship no JavaScript of their own and set no cookies. Page transitions and the short entrance on the home page are CSS, and both are switched off for visitors who prefer reduced motion. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the source is public.
