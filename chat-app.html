Chat App

A mobile chat in React Native with photo and location sharing, readable offline.

A chat app for iOS and Android. You enter a name, pick a background color and start writing. Messages arrive in real time, and you can send a photo from the camera or the library, or your current location.

Real time

Messages are stored in Firebase Firestore and reach every open client as they are written. Sign-in is anonymous, so there is no account to create. Images are uploaded to Firebase Storage.

Offline

The conversation is cached on the device with AsyncStorage. Without a connection you can still read it. The input field disappears until the connection returns, so nobody writes a message that cannot be sent.

Camera and location

Camera, photo library and location go through Expo, each behind its own permission request. Working with them showed me where iOS and Android behave differently.
